# Chroma media preservation

## AX-CHROMA-PRESERVE-MEDIA
Archive source only after verified transparent WebM export and preserve archived originals thereafter. First exports never overwrite unrelated output or archive files. Repeat exports may atomically replace only the editing session's tracked output after verification. Preview and export share the filter builder. Errors and cancellation preserve both source media and any previous verified output.

## AX-CHROMA-REEXPORT-SESSION
Retain the archived source path, exact output path, settings and preview time after export. Further exports replace that tracked WebM and never archive the source again or create a nested chroma-originals folder. Moving to another video is explicit.

- `python3 projects/chroma-webm/test_backend.py`
